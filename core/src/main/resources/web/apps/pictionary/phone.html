How to play

  1. One person taps I'll draw and reads the secret word on their phone.
  2. They tap Start. The drawing shows on the TV and their team shouts guesses.
  3. Someone not drawing taps Got it, on a phone or with OK on the remote.
  4. Guessed in the first 20 seconds: 3 points. After that: 2. Hard words: 1 more.
  5. One free skip per turn. No letters or numbers in the drawing.
  6. Time up: the other teams get 10 seconds to shout, for 1 point.
