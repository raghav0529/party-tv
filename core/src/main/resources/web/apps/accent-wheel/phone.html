        How to play

        1. Spin. The TV picks a voice and a task.
        2. Do the task in that voice until the timer ends.
        3. Everyone votes with one hand: 1 finger is poor, 5 is brilliant. Tap the number most people show.
        4. Commitment +3 if they never dropped the voice. Crack -2 if they burst out laughing.
        5. Someone did it better? Pick them under Stolen by. They get half the points and the performer gets none.
        6. Speed turns the timer to 15 seconds, and it starts by itself.
