    How to play

    1. When a turn starts, one player taps I'm guessing. Everyone else taps Show me the movie.
    2. The team acts it out. No words, no mouthing. Sound effects are fine.
    3. Someone from the other team is the referee: Got it when guessed, Foul if the actors talk.
    4. 60 seconds. Each guess is 1 point. Every 5 in a row gives 3 extra.
    5. The first 2 skips in a turn are free, then each costs 1 point.
    6. The remote works too: OK got it, Right skip, Down foul, Up pause, Left undo. With no phones, press Up on the TV before a turn to show the movie there.
